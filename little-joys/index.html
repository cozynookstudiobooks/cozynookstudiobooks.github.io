About the book

Pip the fox, Bramble the bear, Clover the bunny and Hoot the owl spend cozy days in the woods: baking bread, reading books, splashing in puddles, having tea parties and making pizza together. The book has 48 bold and easy coloring pages with thick outlines and big, simple shapes, so they are easy for small hands and relaxing for grown-ups. Every picture is printed on its own 8.5 x 11 inch page, so markers won't bleed onto the next drawing. Each page has a fun animal or science fact or a cozy caption to read together, and there is a "test your colors" page at the front.

Good for

  • Kids ages 4 to 8 who are learning to color inside the lines
  • Teens and adults who want a calm, low-detail coloring book
  • Markers, crayons and colored pencils (pages are single-sided)
  • Quiet time, travel, rainy days and gifts any time of year

Questions parents ask

What ages is it for?
It is made for kids ages 4 to 8, and many teens and adults use it too because the bold, simple pictures are relaxing to color.
Will markers bleed through?
Every picture is printed single-sided, so even if a marker soaks through, it only reaches the blank back of the page, not the next drawing.
How big are the pages?
8.5 x 11 inches, one picture per page, 48 coloring pages in all.
Is there a printable version?
Yes. A printable PDF version (US Letter and A4) is sold on our Etsy shop: https://www.etsy.com/listing/4596111664
Who are the characters?
Pip the fox, Bramble the bear, Clover the bunny and Hoot the owl, the Cozy Woodland Friends. They appear in every book in the series.
How are the books made?
The line art is made with the help of AI image tools, then each page is checked by a person before it goes into a book. We say so on Amazon and Etsy too.
